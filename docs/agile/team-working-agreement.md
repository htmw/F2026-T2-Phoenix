# Team Working Agreement — Team 2 Phoenix (Agent Mesh)

Working norms for Sprint 0 onward. Formal PDF/Word copies will be uploaded to the course wiki later.

## Communication
- Primary channel: team group chat / GitHub issues and PRs
- Response expectation: within one business day on open questions blocking work
- Meeting cadence: sprint planning, mid-sprint sync, sprint review / retro

## Ownership
- Each member owns the work tied to their role (see wiki Team Members)
- PRs need at least one review before merge to `master`
- No secrets in git; use `.env` locally from `.env.example`

## Quality bar
- Follow Definition of Done: [definition-of-done.md](./definition-of-done.md)
- CI must pass on PRs
- Document non-obvious decisions in `docs/` or ADRs

## Conflict & escalation
- Raise blockers early in chat or standup
- Escalate to Team Leader (Dhwani) if unresolved within a sprint day
- Advisor (Prof. Henry Wong) for technical AI/ML guidance
